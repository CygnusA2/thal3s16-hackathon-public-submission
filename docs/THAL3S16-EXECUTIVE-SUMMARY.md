# THAL3S 16 — Executive Summary

**Cygnus-A² | Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026**

## The problem and the solution
Wealthy people can own valuable property yet have limited cash. Assessing collateral requires reliable valuations, debt information, uncertainty analysis and human review. **THAL3S 16** brings this information into one reviewable experience. A deterministic financial engine calculates illustrative haircuts, effective LTV and indicative liquidity. A Meta model interprets bounded evidence and flags gaps; it does not approve credit or authorize funding.

## Two innovations
**Collateral intelligence:** a seven-stage assessment from property selection through evidence, valuation, haircut, LTV and indicative capacity to human review.

**Financial Intelligence Protocol (FIP):** numerical codes and strict structured outputs address ambiguity in AI-generated financial prose. Trusted software owns authoritative facts and calculations; the model contributes interpretation.

## Measured research
Live FIP research used **Muse Spark Open**, a Meta model served through **Fireworks AI**. In the three main FIP V2 studies, **70/93** synthetic attempts validated (75.3%). Blocked-status: **50/50**; clean-status: **20/43**. Conditional classification and evidence-citation precision among validated cases were 100%. Median study latency: **8.22 seconds**. Rejected cases exposed model-versus-engine severity disagreements rather than being silently accepted.

The 96-cell synthetic grid had at least one live measurement per cell: 94 FIP V2 and two earlier narrative-era. This does **not** establish real-borrower performance, validated token savings, model superiority or liquidity delivered in under 15 minutes. No credit approval, issuance or funding was performed.

**Principle: AI interprets; verified software calculates; humans decide.**
