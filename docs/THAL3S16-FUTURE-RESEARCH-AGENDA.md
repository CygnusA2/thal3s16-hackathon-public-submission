# THAL3S 16 — Future Research Agenda

*Founder-authored forward agenda, documented as part of the final
submission freeze (2026-11-02). Every item below is a FUTURE objective:
none is implemented, measured, or claimed as a current capability.*

1. **384-variant robustness study.** The frozen 384-case fixture
   contains ~4 evidence/leverage variants of each of its 96 base
   cells. A variant-level study would re-measure measured cells to
   quantify within-cell robustness (variance across evidence
   configurations of the same borrower scenario) — explicitly a
   robustness measurement, not new independent scenarios.
2. **A future population of 384 genuinely distinct synthetic
   scenarios.** Expanding the authored grid beyond 96 base cells to
   384 distinct borrower scenarios (new profiles, portfolio
   structures, evidence regimes) so that a full-scale study measures
   384 independent units rather than variant repeats.
3. **UHNW borrower risk preferences.** Modeling and measuring how
   ultra-high-net-worth borrower preferences (liquidity horizons,
   collateral attachment tolerance, concentration limits) should
   condition the deterministic engine's illustrative parameters.
4. **Collateral intelligence across additional illiquid assets.**
   Extending the evidence pipeline beyond luxury real estate to
   other illiquid asset classes (private equity, concentrated equity,
   art/collectibles proxies) with the same verified-fact-projection
   discipline.
5. **Economic-cycle research.** Deepening the six-regime economic
   framework with additional historical macro snapshots, cycle
   sequencing and regime-transition stress paths.
6. **Multi-model evaluations.** Running the frozen FIP V2 contract
   and the 96-cell census design against additional Meta model routes
   (Muse Spark 1.3, Muse Wonder 1.0 via Meta Model API) once
   provisioned — enabling genuine paired model comparisons, which the
   single-model program to date explicitly does not support.
7. **Actual end-to-end liquidity execution studies.** Measuring what
   a real liquidity transaction pipeline would require (timelines,
   counterparties, documentation, settlement) — a business/market
   study, not a benchmark; no such execution is implemented or claimed.

**Objective/unproven markers (submission freeze):** token savings and
total-cost reductions relative to the narrative contract are
*objectives* — output sizes were measured (568 vs 1,992 bytes offline;
compact live documents), but statistically validated savings were not
established. Production credit approval, funding authorization and
liquidity in under 15 minutes are **not demonstrated and not
claimed**: every financial decision in THAL3S 16 remains deterministic
with mandatory human review, and every benchmark result is an
authored-synthetic-population measurement.
