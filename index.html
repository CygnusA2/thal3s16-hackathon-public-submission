SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Research-grounded assumptions, not random client figures

The client profiles are deliberately constructed synthetic examples. The cited research informs wealth tiers and portfolio categories; it does not provide these exact individual values or county averages.

  1. Altrata — World Ultra Wealth Report 2026UHNW definitions, population and asset-holding patterns.Report ↗

  3. Knight Frank — The Wealth Report 2026Global luxury-real-estate and wealth context.Report ↗
  4. PPIC — Wealth in CaliforniaIndependent California household-wealth distribution benchmark.Research ↗
How the synthetic figures are modelled

Net worth = property assets + other assets − secured mortgage − other liabilities. Portfolio collateral capacity = max(0, property value × (1 − haircut) × combined-LTV limit − existing property debt). These are demonstration assumptions, not lender offers or verified observations.

No verified client liabilities, credit score, cash-flow assessment, title verification or real customer records are used. THAL3S still requires human review and does not authorize credit, issuance or funding.
